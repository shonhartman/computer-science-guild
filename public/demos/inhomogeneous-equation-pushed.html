Linear Differential Equations — §1

The Swing That Someone Keeps Pushing

Left alone, a swing just swings and settles. Push it on a rhythm, and its motion changes shape entirely.

y″ + 3y′ + 2y = sin(x)
left side: the swing's own physics
right side: an outside push
the push depends on x, not on y

Question: once something is pushing back, how do you solve for the motion?

The reveal

It's two solutions wearing one equation

y = yc + yp

yc is how the system behaves with no push at all. yp is just enough extra motion to account for the push.

Set the right side to zero and you get the homogeneous equation — solved first, always, because yc is baked into every answer.

Why it splits this way

Linearity means the pieces don't interfere

L[y] = L[yc] + L[yp] = 0 + g(x)

Because L (the derivative-and-add operator) is linear, applying it to a sum equals the sum of applying it to each part. That's the whole trick.

Finding y_p

Plug the guess back into the equation, match up matching terms on both sides, and solve for the coefficients. If the guess overlaps yc, multiply it by x and try again.

Worked example

Same swing. Same rhythmic push.

y″ + 3y′ + 2y = sin(x)
↓
yc = C1e−x + C2e−2x  (from α²+3α+2=0)
↓
yp = (1/10)sin(x) − (3/10)cos(x)
↓
y = C1e−x + C2e−2x + (1/10)sin(x) − (3/10)cos(x)

As x grows, the e−x and e−2x terms decay to nothing — only the sine-driven y_p survives. The push outlasts the free swing.

Why it matters beyond swings

Something pushing on a system? Split it in two.

🔌

RLC circuits

An applied voltage is the forcing function; the circuit's own resistance and reactance give y_c.

🚗

Car suspensions

Bumps in the road force the system; the shock absorber's natural damping is the homogeneous part.

📈

Forced economies

A one-time stimulus is g(x); the market's own supply-demand dynamics are the free response.

No outside force, no independent-variable term on the right — homogeneous, and the system just does what it does. Add a forcing function, and the answer becomes: what it would do anyway, plus what the push demands.
