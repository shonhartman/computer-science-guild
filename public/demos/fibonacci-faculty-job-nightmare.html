The Graduate Student Job Nightmare

10,000 CS faculty jobs. Fixed. No new lines ever created.

10,000total faculty jobs

Question: how long until the market is completely saturated?

The reveal

It's Fibonacci in a lab coat

Same sequence as rabbit breeding — 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13…

Documented in India around 200 BC for poetic meter, centuries before Fibonacci's rabbits in 1202.

Why it's Fibonacci

Each year leans on the two before it

f(n) = f(n−1) + f(n−2)

This year's job-seekers = last year's total professors (they just ripened) + the year before's.

The magic trick

Guess an exponential. Let algebra do the work.

f(n) = αn  —  try it and see
↓
αn = αn−1 + αn−2
↓
α² − α − 1 = 0  (the characteristic equation)
↓
α = φ = 1.618…  or  α = ψ = −0.618…

Nobody asked for the golden ratio. It just falls out of the math — unforced.

The shortcut & the punchline

No more counting. Just plug in n.

f(n) = (φn − ψn) / √5

Binet's formula — an irrational-looking expression that always lands on a whole number.

~20years to saturate the market
