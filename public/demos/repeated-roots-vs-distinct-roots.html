Linear Recurrences: The Century Plant

Repeated Roots (Linear) vs. Distinct Roots (Exponential)

The Century Plant (Repeated Root)

  • Formula: f(n) = 2f(n-1) - f(n-2)
  • Polynomial: (α - 1)² = 0
  • Root Multiplicity: Double root at α = 1
  • Fate: Collapses to Linear Growth [f(n) = n]

Fibonacci (Distinct Roots)

  • Formula: F(n) = F(n-1) + F(n-2)
  • Polynomial: α² - α - 1 = 0
  • Roots: Two distinct real roots
  • Fate: Explodes into Exponential Growth
